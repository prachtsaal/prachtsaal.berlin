Anthropobsceno, by Argentine artist Seudo, is the fourth part of a five-part series exploring the relationship between humanity, nature, and the Anthropocene.

The work revisits ancestral Inca knowledge and sustainable terrace farming, bringing it into dialogue with contemporary technologies and today’s environmental challenges.

It reflects on how ancestral knowledge can inspire new ways of caring for the Earth—and on the importance of giving communities their own voice.
