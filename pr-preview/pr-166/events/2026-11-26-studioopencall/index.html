Studio open call

Shared studio available for rent now

2026-11-26

We’re looking for new people to join us at Prachtsaal Studio, our beautiful open space in Neukölln (halfway between U-Leinestraße and U-Hermannstraße)!

Prachtsaal is a non-profit with about 20 resident artists in various fields. The studio is ~342m² and is divided in 10 ateliers. Once in a while we run events, but otherwise it's quiet.

We have a nice garden, a small kitchen, a shed with tools, a laser cutter, a 3D-printer, large format printers, projectors, speakers, and many nice people :-)

The studio is at the street level, the bathrooms are downstairs. The available space for rent is located in a mezzanine inside Prachtsaal Studio, accessible via a spiral staircase.

The 17.6m² studio and the 4.4m² balcony are shared with Marta. Each one pays ~270€ rent per month.

On the top left you can see the North side of the mezzanine, a wall of tinted glass.

Another view of the inside.

If you are interested, please get in touch via e-mail or Instagram and arrange a visit <3
